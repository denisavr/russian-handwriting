# Pilot: low-resolution natural handwriting, 2026-10-29

Status: two fresh reading passes completed; **not an accepted accuracy benchmark**.
Same parent-inherited model and effort, fresh contexts, same Russian request and image.
Exact model identifier and sampling settings are not exposed by the subagent interface.
Baseline could inspect only the image; skill condition could additionally inspect the
skill and its references. Keys, web search, memory and other outputs were prohibited.
This was instruction-based isolation in a shared filesystem, not an access-control sandbox.

Image: [Anton Chekhov autograph, 1890](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Чехов_автограф.jpg),
446×700 original JPEG, distinct document and author from the reference library.
Commons marks PD-old / PD-RusEmpire / US expired; downloaded outside the library.
Chinese text and the upside-down top line were excluded by the shared prompt.
A Tolstoy letter considered earlier was rejected after visual inspection: it is mostly
typewritten and cannot stand in for a natural-handwriting benchmark.

Both passes abstained on much of the page. The skill pass gave more tentative readings
and a targeted month question, but also called several incorrect phrases relatively
confident. For example, it read the first verb as «начерталъ» and later read «живу»;
the published letter has «начертаны» and «плыву». The baseline's first-word reading
«Сын» also disagrees with the published «Сии». These are observed failures, not a
numerical estimate of general error rates.

The published letter (Chekhov letters, volume 4, letter 847 to family, 1 July 1890)
is independently available in scholarly print and electronic editions. It is useful
for checking content, but its normalized typography/spelling and absent manuscript
line boundaries do not yet constitute a fully verified literal key. No CER or entity
accuracy is reported from a guessed key. Abstention span lengths also require alignment.
Reference: [electronic edition](https://predanie.ru/book/221236-pisma-1890-1892/full/),
letter 847; primary scholarly scan at
[IMLI](https://biblio.imli.ru/images/abook/russliteratura/Chehov_PSSiP_v_30_tt_S_v_18tt_S_t_21_P_t_04_1976.pdf).

Next: freeze a verified literal key and scored regions before a new paired run, record
the full prompts and raw output hashes, and align abstentions/entities. Do not use
this now-seen Chekhov page as a fresh blind test for the same reading contexts.
A single public literary page also has pretraining contamination risk.
